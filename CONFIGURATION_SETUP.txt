CAU HINH DU AN VA KET QUA RA SOAT HARDCODE
=========================================

Nguon cau hinh
--------------
util.AppConfig doc gia tri theo thu tu:
1. Bien moi truong cua tien trinh Tomcat.
2. JVM system property (-DKEY=value).
3. File UTF-8 duoc chi dinh boi MENSFORM_CONFIG_FILE.
4. src/java/application.properties: gia tri mac dinh khong chua bi mat.

Neu khong chi dinh MENSFORM_CONFIG_FILE, ung dung tim lan luot:
- ${catalina.base}/conf/mensform.properties cua Tomcat.
- config/application.local.properties tu thu muc lam viec cua tien trinh.
- config/application.local.properties cua project chua thu muc classes dang
  duoc NetBeans trien khai (nhan dien bang build.xml va src/java).
Duong dan duoc chi dinh ro qua MENSFORM_CONFIG_FILE luon duoc uu tien; neu
file do khong doc duoc thi bao loi, khong tu dong chuyen sang file khac.
Khoi dong lai NetBeans/Tomcat sau khi thay doi cau hinh.

Cau hinh tren may nay
--------------------
Thong tin database truoc day trong DBConnection.java da duoc chuyen sang
config/application.local.properties. File nay duoc Git bo qua, khong nam trong
web/ va khong duoc dong goi vao WAR.

Mo PowerShell tai thu muc du an va chay de IDE/Tomcat tim dung file:

  setx MENSFORM_CONFIG_FILE (Resolve-Path config/application.local.properties).Path

Sau do dong/mo lai NetBeans va khoi dong lai Tomcat. Bien moi truong DB_* da
co tren may se uu tien hon gia tri trong file. Khong chia se file local.

Tren may khac, sao chep config/application.example.properties thanh file
cau hinh rieng va dien DB_HOST, DB_PORT, DB_NAME, DB_USER, DB_PASSWORD.
DB_JDBC_URL tuy chon cho ket noi SQL Server can tham so bo sung; thong tin
dang nhap van duoc doc rieng tu DB_USER va DB_PASSWORD.
DB_TRUST_SERVER_CERTIFICATE mac dinh false; chi bat khi can cho SQL Server
noi bo voi chung chi tu ky. Khong co tai khoan/mat khau database mac dinh.

Cac nhom da chuyen khoi ma nguon
-------------------------------
- DB_*: may chu, cong, database, tai khoan, TLS, timeout va connection pool.
- APP_NAME, APP_LOCALE, APP_CURRENCY, APP_TIMEZONE: ten hien thi va dinh dang.
  APP_CURRENCY chi dieu khien hien thi; luong thanh toan VNPay hien dung VND.
- MAIL_*, GMAIL_*: SMTP, cong, timeout, EHLO, ten nguoi gui va thong tin xac thuc.
  STORE_ORDER_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL la hop thu nhan thong bao cua cua hang.
  Email khach va chi tiet san pham duoc truy van theo don hang trong database.
- VNPAY_*: URL Sandbox, merchant credentials, loai don, ngon ngu, thoi han.
- GHN_*: API staging, shop/token, cache, chu ky dong bo, worker, trong luong,
  kich thuoc kien, loai dich vu, ghi chu giao hang va gioi han COD.
  URL van duoc kiem tra dung host staging/Sandbox truoc khi gui credentials.
- SHIPPING_* va SHOP_ADDRESS: nguong mien phi, phi toi da va dia chi cua hang.
  Bieu phi uoc tinh J&T cu va cac ham tao don bang phi mac dinh da duoc xoa.
  Checkout lay phi tu GHN Test; khi khong co bao gia hop le thi dung dat hang.
  JSP va JavaScript lay nguong/can nang/kich thuoc tu cung cau hinh Java.
- GEMINI_*, CHATBOT_*: endpoint/model, prompt, token, timeout, retry,
  gioi han tin nhan/lich su/tan suat, so ket qua va tu dien mau.
- OUTFIT_*: tu dien phan loai/mau, quy tac cham diem, ngan sach phu kien
  va so luong goi y. San pham, gia va ton kho lay tu database.
- FIT_*: quy tac size tham khao cua cong cu tu van tren trang chu.
- AUTH_*, SESSION_*, PASSWORD_HASH_ITERATIONS: han OTP, gui lai, so lan thu,
  thoi gian phien va chi phi tao password hash. Hash cu van xac minh duoc.
- PRODUCT_IMAGE_*: so anh, dung luong, pixel, chat luong JPG va gioi han upload.
  Cac servlet multipart va JavaScript deu nhan gioi han tu cau hinh chung.
- CART_MAX_ITEM_QUANTITY, INVENTORY_MAX_*, COLLECTION_MAX_BUDGET,
  HOME_PRODUCT_LIMIT, ORDER_TRACKING_*: gioi han va chu ky hien thi nghiep vu.
- UI_FONT_STYLESHEET_URL: nguon font chung; anh placeholder la asset rieng.

Ghi chu pham vi
---------------
web/META-INF/context.xml phai giu thuoc tinh Context path de NetBeans trien
khai ung dung. Day la cau hinh deployment; bo thuoc tinh nay se lam NetBeans
bao loi path null ngay ca khi Java/JSP da bien dich thanh cong.

Ten bang/cot, SQL tham so hoa, route, trang thai/role theo schema, ma giao thuc,
don vi do luong, gioi han an toan cua API/crypto, noi dung giao dien va gia tri
CSS/SVG la dinh nghia chuong trinh. Cac gia tri nay duoc giu theo hop dong ky
thuat; khong bien quyen truy cap hay schema thanh cau hinh tuy y.
Du lieu seed va migration SQL la du lieu khoi tao, khong phai du lieu gia
lap duoc tra ve thay cho database trong luong chay thuc te.

Kiem tra
--------
Script scripts/run-config-checks.ps1 nhan -TomcatHome va -AntHome (Ant tuy chon).
Script bien dich Java, chay kiem tra cau hinh/payment/carrier/Tomcat, kiem tra
cu phap JavaScript va PowerShell; neu co Ant thi bien dich toan bo JSP.
Cac kiem tra dung du lieu gia va carrier stub, khong gui Gmail, khong goi
API thanh toan/van chuyen that, khong thay doi du lieu kinh doanh.

Vi du (thay duong dan bang ban cai tren may):
  ./scripts/run-config-checks.ps1 -TomcatHome $env:CATALINA_HOME -AntHome $env:ANT_HOME

Khong commit file local, .env, token, App Password hoac credentials.

Dieu huong mot dia chi
---------------------
APP_SINGLE_URL_ENABLED=true giu thanh dia chi tai context root cua ung dung.
Filter SingleUrlNavigationFilter chi boc GET dieu huong trang cua trinh duyet
vao app-shell.jsp; POST, fetch, iframe, assets va VNPay IPN van di truc tiep
den controller. Moi trang ben trong van thuc hien day du kiem tra quyen.
Trang hien tai duoc luu trong history.state cua tung tab de F5 mo lai dung
trang, khong luu vao session dung chung giua cac tab. Back/Forward dung
lich su dieu huong iframe cua trinh duyet.

VNPay phai mo o cua so chinh de khong bi nha cung cap chan nhung iframe.
Khi quay ve, callback giu nguyen chu ky/tham so va duoc xu ly trong trang
ben trong. Chuc nang nay an URL khoi thanh dia chi, khong che request hay
ma HTML/JavaScript khoi Developer Tools. Sao chep URL root se mo trang chu.
Dat APP_SINGLE_URL_ENABLED=false va khoi dong lai de tro ve dieu huong cu.

Kiem tra Chrome read-only (can Playwright; NODE_PATH neu thu vien o ngoai repo):
  $env:MENSFORM_BASE_URL = 'http://localhost:8080/MenWearStore/'
  $env:MENSFORM_BROWSER_EXECUTABLE = '<duong dan Chrome tren may>'
  node test/web/single-url-navigation.browser.cjs
