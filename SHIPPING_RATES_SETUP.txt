PHI GIAO HANG GHN TEST
=====================

Checkout lay phi tu API GHN Test theo dia chi giao hang. Bieu phi J&T uoc tinh
cu va cac ham tao don bang phi mac dinh da duoc xoa khoi ung dung.

Cau hinh xem CONFIGURATION_SETUP.txt va src/java/application.properties:
- SHIPPING_FREE_THRESHOLD: nguong mien phi theo gia tri tien hang.
- SHIPPING_MAX_FEE: gioi han kiem tra bao gia van chuyen.
- SHOP_ADDRESS: dia chi hien thi cua cua hang.
- GHN_ESTIMATED_ITEM_WEIGHT_GRAMS: trong luong uoc tinh cua moi san pham.
- GHN_ESTIMATED_LENGTH_CM/WIDTH_CM/HEIGHT_CM: kich thuoc kien uoc tinh.
- GHN_TOKEN/GHN_SHOP_ID: tai khoan va diem lay hang tu GHN.

JSP, JavaScript va Java deu lay chinh sach tu cau hinh chung. Khi dia chi hoac
bao gia GHN khong hop le, checkout yeu cau tinh lai va khong tu gan phi mac dinh.
Don du dieu kien mien phi van phai co dia chi duoc GHN xac nhan.
Kho can can do kien thuc te truoc khi tao van don.

Thong tin secret luu trong bien moi truong hoac file cau hinh local duoc Git
bo qua. Khoi dong lai NetBeans/Tomcat sau khi thay doi cau hinh.
