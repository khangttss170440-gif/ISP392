- Checkout lay phi truc tiep tu GHN test, khong am tham dung lai bang gia J&T.

  Khong tu gan ma quan/phuong hoac dung dia chi nguoi nhan lam dia chi gui.
  Neu GHN van bao thieu dia chi gui, cap nhat dia chi lay hang trong Quan ly cua hang GHN.
- Loi API hien thi ten buoc (tinh/quan/phuong/cua hang/tinh phi) va mo ta GHN da an Token.
  Khong the ket luan nguyen nhan chi tu HTTP 400; can xem thong bao cu the.
- Don tu 799000d (tien hang truoc coupon) mien phi ship, van doi chieu dia chi GHN.
- Hien dung danh muc dia chi GHN cu co phuong/xa + quan/huyen + tinh/thanh,
  phu hop So dia chi hien tai. Ten phai khop duy nhat; ten mo ho/khong khop bi tu choi.
  Chua ho tro chuyen doi tu dong dia chi hanh chinh moi 2 cap sang dia chi cu.
- Uoc tinh 500g/san pham, kich thuoc 30x20x10cm. Toi da 39 san pham/19.5kg o checkout.
- Kho can nhap can nang, kich thuoc thuc te (kien duoi 20kg) truoc khi tao van don.
- Sales xac nhan don nhu cu; kho vao Chi tiet don -> Tao van don GHN test.
- COD thu dung Payment.Amount (da bao gom phi ship khach tra), VNPay Paid thu ho 0d.
  Shop tra phi GHN (payment_type_id=1), tranh thu them phi van chuyen lan hai.
- Phi hang o kho co the khac checkout do can nang/kich thuoc thuc; shop chiu chenh lech.
- Tao van don xong: Shipment Preparing, SalesOrder van Confirmed.
- Nut Cap nhat trang thai tu GHN doi soat bang API. Khong tu danh dau giao thanh cong
  chi vi tao van don thanh cong. Khi picked/di chuyen -> Shipped; delivered -> Delivered
  va Payment COD Pending -> Paid. Trang thai tra hang/that lac khong bi doi thanh Delivered.
- Dong bo phi bang API /shipping-order/soc cua van don da ton tai; neu API phi gap loi,
  van giu trang thai da dong bo va phi da luu. Chua co phi se hien thong bao ro, khong coi la 0d.
- Co dong bo chay nen khi Tomcat hoat dong: moi van don dang xu ly duoc tra cuu GHN
  khoang 60 giay/lan, khong can kho bam nut va khong can ngrok cho viec tra cuu.
- Tien trinh quet hang doi nhe moi 5 giay, nhung moi don chi duoc goi GHN theo moc NextSyncAt
  cach lan bat dau truoc 60 giay. Co 4 worker va hang doi gioi han de khong lam cham frontend.
- Khoa SyncLeaseToken/SyncLeaseUntil tren SQL Server ngan nhieu Tomcat/nhieu nut bam dong bo
  cung mot don. Khi loi giu du lieu cu, ghi so lan loi va thu lai sau 60 giay; khong tao van don moi.
- Dung dong bo tu dong cho delivered/cancel/returned/exception/lost/damage/scrap va don noi bo da huy.
  Nut cap nhat thu cong van dung duoc khi can doi soat phi/trang thai.
- LastSyncedAt luu UTC. Trang khach hien thoi gian theo Viet Nam.
- Don hang cua toi va Chi tiet don hang tu cap nhat moi phut khi tab dang mo. Khi quay lai tab,
  web doc lai ngay tu database; khong goi GHN bang trinh duyet. Endpoint kiem tra quyen so huu

- Dong bo khong tu tao trang thai mo phong: GHN test van ready_to_pick thi web van cho lay hang.

5. WEBHOOK GHN TEST (TUY CHON, VAN GIU DONG BO MOI PHUT)
- Endpoint da duoc them: POST /MenWearStore/webhooks/ghn
- Dung URL HTTPS cua ngrok dang online + duong dan tren, khong nhap localhost tren GHN.
- Chay scripts/configure-ghn-webhook.ps1 tren may chay Tomcat.
  Script sinh/giu lai GHN_WEBHOOK_SECRET, luu vao User environment va copy secret vao clipboard;
  khong hien secret, khong dung GHN_TOKEN lam webhook secret, khong ghi vao Git.
- Dong/mo lai NetBeans va Tomcat de nhan GHN_WEBHOOK_SECRET.
- Tai developer.ghn.dev -> Cau hinh webhook -> Order:
  URL Endpoint: https://TEN_MIEN_NGROK_DANG_CHAY/MenWearStore/webhooks/ghn
  Giu header Content-Type = application/json.
  Them header X-GHN-Webhook-Secret = gia tri clipboard (Ctrl+V).
  Chon create, switch_status, update_fee (co the giu cac su kien Order mac dinh).
  Timeout 10 giay, retry 3. Bam Tao webhook/Luu tren GHN.
- Theo tai lieu GHN, cau hinh thay doi co the can khoang 15 phut de co hieu luc.
- GET URL chi xac nhan route co ton tai; POST phai co header secret moi duoc nhan.
- Chi nhan dung ShopID test, OrderCode + ClientOrderCode cua don thuoc ung dung.
  Callback khong dung pham vi duoc acknowledge va bo qua; Token/API secret khong duoc ghi log.
- Khu trung lap ben vung bang SHA-256(OrderCode|Type|Time), luu GhnWebhookReceipt (khong luu payload).
- Webhook chi danh dau don can dong bo; worker thuong tra cuu lai trong vai giay. Trang thai,
  COD va phi duoc doc lai tu API GHN, khong lay truc tiep tu payload callback.
- Neu API/website loi, dong bo moi phut van la du phong. Webhook khong mo phong giao hang thanh cong.
