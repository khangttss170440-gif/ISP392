THU MUC ANH SAN PHAM MENSFORM

Moi san pham co mot thu muc theo Product ID, vi du:
  product-images/12/00-cover.jpg
  product-images/12/01-front.jpg
  product-images/12/02-back.png

Thay 12 bang Product ID that trong danh sach san pham.
Ban co the tu tao thu muc va chep anh JPG/PNG vao day.
Ten file chi dung chu cai khong dau, so, dau gach ngang, gach duoi va dau cham.
Anh dau tien theo thu tu ten file se la anh dai dien. Nen dat 00-cover.jpg.

Admin va nhan vien kho co the chon toi da 6 anh khi tao san pham.
Admin va nhan vien kho co the vao muc Anh san pham de tai them, thay, xoa
va chon anh dai dien. Tong toi da 6 anh, thay anh khong tang so luong.
Moi anh toi da 5 MB, 12 megapixel. Chi ho tro JPG va PNG.
Thu muc Product ID duoc tu tao khi luu san pham, ke ca khi chua chon anh.
Co the thay/xoa anh cu truc tiep tren trang Anh san pham hoac trong thu muc.
Anh tai len tu form duoc dat ten tu dong; anh cover-* la anh dai dien mac dinh.
Khi da chon anh dai dien tren giao dien, file .cover luu lua chon nay.
Neu chi quan ly bang thu muc, xoa file .cover de dung lai thu tu ten file,
roi dat anh mong muon thanh 00-cover.jpg hoac 00-cover.png.

Anh luu ngoai web/ va WAR, nen khong bi xoa khi Clean and Build/deploy lai.
Can sao luu thu muc nay cung voi du lieu san pham.
May hien tai: web/WEB-INF/web.xml cau hinh D:/ISP392/ISP392/product-images.
Khi chuyen may, doi productImageDirectory trong web.xml hoac dat bien moi truong
MENSFORM_PRODUCT_IMAGE_DIR thanh duong dan tuyet doi. Bien moi truong duoc uu tien.
Tomcat can quyen doc/ghi thu muc do. Khoi dong lai Tomcat sau khi doi cau hinh.

ANH THEO MAU BIEN THE
Admin/Kho: Anh san pham -> chon Product ID -> chon Mau trong anh -> Luu mau anh.
Danh sach mau lay tu SKU / Bien the cua dung Product ID. Them SKU truoc neu chua co mau.
Co the gan nhieu anh cho cung mot mau; cac size cung mau dung chung nhom anh.
Chon Anh chung / moi mau de dung anh cho tat ca mau.
File .colors tu luu lien ket anh-mau (UTF-8); can sao luu cung voi anh.
Thay anh tren giao dien se giu mau cu. Xoa anh se xoa lien ket mau cua anh do.
Anh khong co lien ket mau van hien thi nhu anh chung, khong can doi ten hay chuyen thu muc.
