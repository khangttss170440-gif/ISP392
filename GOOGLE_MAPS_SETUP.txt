MENSFORM - GOOGLE MAPS VA PHI GIAO HANG

Dia chi shop: 138 Quang Trung, khu pho 7, thi tran Hoc Mon, TP. Ho Chi Minh, Viet Nam.
Khoang cach la ban kinh duong thang (Haversine), khong phai quang duong lai xe.
Tu shop den dia chi giao hang <= 5 km: 0 dong. Ngoai 5 km: 45.000 dong toan quoc.
Phi luu trong SalesOrder.ShippingFee va cong vao Payment.Amount ngay khi tao don.
Coupon chi giam tien hang; khong giam phi giao hang.

KHI CHUA CO API KEY
- So dia chi va checkout co ban do xem dia chi va nut mo Google Maps.
- Chua the tu xac minh toa do/ban kinh. Khong tu gan toa do gan dung cho shop.
- Giao dien bao chua xac minh khoang cach, phi tam tinh 45.000 dong.
- Phi duoc kiem tra lai tren server khi dat hang. Trinh duyet khong quyet dinh phi.

DE BAT TIM TOA DO VA MIEN PHI TU DONG TRONG 5 KM
1. Tao Google Cloud project, lien ket billing va bat Geocoding API.
2. Tao API key rieng cho server, gioi han chi Geocoding API va IP server khi trien khai.
3. Dat bien moi truong GOOGLE_MAPS_SERVER_API_KEY tren may chay Tomcat.
4. Neu muon ve vong tron 5 km truc tiep trong trang: bat Maps JavaScript API,
   tao key trinh duyet rieng GOOGLE_MAPS_BROWSER_API_KEY, gioi han referrer
   http://localhost:8080/* khi phat trien va domain that khi trien khai.
5. Khoi dong lai Tomcat (va NetBeans neu Tomcat duoc mo tu NetBeans) de nhan bien moi truong.

Neu Google khong dinh vi chinh xac dia chi shop, mo vi tri shop tren Google Maps,
kiem tra ghim dung so nha, roi dat SHOP_LATITUDE va SHOP_LONGITUDE theo ghim da xac minh.
Khong co toa do mac dinh gia dinh. Khong tu nhan toa do do khach hang gui len de mien phi ship.
Toa do Google chi dung cho lan tinh phi, khong ghi lau dai vao database.

Su dung: Tai khoan -> So dia chi -> Them/Sua -> nhap dia chi -> Tim dia chi & tinh phi.
Checkout: chon dia chi da luu hoac nhap dia chi moi, bam tinh phi truoc khi dat hang.
Neu phi thay doi khi server kiem tra lai, don khong duoc tao va khach duoc yeu cau xem lai tong tien.
