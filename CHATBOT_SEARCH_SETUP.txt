CHATBOT TIM SAN PHAM VA BO SUU TAP
=================================

Vi du co the thu
----------------
- tim dong ho
- Tim ao so mi duoi 500k
- Con mau den size L khong?
- Tim do di lam gom quan ao giay dong ho ca vatk
- Tim bo suu tap di lam
- Tim bo suu tap cuoi tuan duoi 5 trieu
- Tim bo suu tap co dong ho
- Tim bo co giay size 42
- Con duoi 2 trieu?
- Khong gioi han gia
- Xem bo suu tap khac
- Tim san pham moi

Nguon du lieu
-------------
San pham, mau, size, SKU, gia va ton kho lay truc tiep tu cac ban ghi Active
trong SQL Server. Mau/size moi duoc doc tu danh muc. Ket qua gom theo san pham
de khong chiem het danh sach bang cac size cua cung mot mau.

Bo suu tap chi lay ban ghi Published. Moi mon trong bo phai con ban va con
hang o dung mau da duoc cua hang chon. Bo nhap, da an, thieu san pham hoac het
mot mon se khong duoc gioi thieu. Khong tu tao bo suu tap gia neu khong tim thay.

Ngan sach bo suu tap duoc so voi TONG gia cac mon, tu phien ban con hang re
nhat khop bo loc. Gia thuc te co the thay doi khi chon mau/size tren trang chi
tiet. Link cua chatbot mo dung bo da chon qua collectionId tren /collections.

Hoi tiep va nhieu nhom san pham
------------------------------
Bo loc duoc luu theo session, khong dung chung giua khach. Hoi mau/size/gia
se giu cac dieu kien khac. Doi loai san pham hoac doi giua san pham/bo suu tap
se bat dau pham vi phu hop; reset xoa ca lich su va bo loc.

Yeu cau nhieu nhom (ao, quan, giay, dong ho, ca vat) duoc phan bo ket qua theo
tung nhom, khong lap cung san pham. Neu chua co mon khop thi thong bao nhom
con thieu. Goi y san pham le khong duoc trinh bay nhu mot bo suu tap da dang.

Xu ly cau hoi
-------------
Ho tro tieng Viet khong dau, tu dong nghia, sai chinh ta nhe cua ten san pham,
gia dang 300-500k, tu 300 den 500k, 1.500.000, 1tr5 va ngan sach gan dung.
Gia/mau/size khong duoc tu dong noi long khi khong tim thay. Giao dien hien
thi bo loc dang ap dung va nut bo dieu kien de khach tu chon.

Tra cuu san pham va bo suu tap khong phu thuoc Gemini. Gemini duoc dung cho
phan tu van khi can, co timeout rieng; neu API ban thi ket qua that van hien.
Khong gui SQL do AI tao vao database.

Cau hinh
--------
CHATBOT_* trong src/java/application.properties dieu khien gioi han ket qua,
tu dien dong nghia, dip su dung, nguong sua loi nho, timeout va gioi han truy van.
GEMINI_* duoc doc qua AppConfig. Credentials nam trong bien moi truong/file
local; khong ghi vao Java/JSP. Khoi dong lai ung dung sau khi doi cau hinh.

CHATBOT_CLIENT_TIMEOUT_MILLIS duoc xuat ngay tren phan tu chatbot trong JSP.
JavaScript chi dat bo dem khi gia tri la so duong hop le; trang cu thieu
cau hinh se khong tu huy yeu cau ngay. Khi deploy can dong bo JSP va assets.

Danh muc hien duoc doc moi moi lan tim, co gioi han CHATBOT_CATALOG_VARIANT_LIMIT
de gioi han bo nho; neu vuot gioi han thi bao loi thay vi tra ket qua bi cat ngam.
Neu danh muc tang lon can chuyen phan ung vien sang chi muc tim kiem database.

Kiem tra
--------
scripts/run-config-checks.ps1 gom kiem tra parser, ngu canh, phan bo nhom,
bo suu tap cong khai/ton kho/ngan sach, HTTP session va bien dich Java/JSP.
test/web/chatbot-timeout.spec.cjs kiem tra timeout, cau hinh trang cu,
loi mang va hien thi ket qua san pham/bo suu tap tren giao dien.
Kiem tra tu dong dung fixture/stub, khong thay doi du lieu kinh doanh.
